## 🎯 TONE TRANSFORMATION

### What Was Removed ❌
- Corporate/sales language
- Years and metrics as credibility markers
- Institutional framing
- Teaching promises ("I teach X developers")
- Exaggerated business impact claims
- Sales conversion messaging
- Authority-based positioning

### What Was Added ✅
- Personal, human voice
- Focus on problems solved
- Learning-centered outcomes
- Authentic "in progress" status
- Community connection language
- Transparent about evolution
- Technical depth without bragging
- Invitation to collaborate
