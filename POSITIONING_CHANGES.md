# Positioning Changes: Before & After

## HERO SECTION

### Before
```
Tagline: "Building | Sharing | Growing"
Role: "Senior .NET Developer & Systems Engineer"
Stats: 
  - 11+ Years Experience
  - FinTech Enterprise Systems
  - 20+ Engineers Mentored
Description: "11+ years building enterprise and financial systems. Sharing real-world experience..."
Badges: Real Experience | Under Construction | No Hype
CTAs: "View my experience" | "Join the newsletter"
```

### After
```
Tagline: "Full Stack Developer"
Role: ".NET · MAUI · React"
Stats:
  - Systems Engineer | Full Stack Developer
  - Open Source | Community Focused
  - Building | Educational Content
Subtitle: "I enjoy technical challenges, building useful software, and sharing the journey of programming and development."
Badges: Authentic | In Progress | Developer-Focused
CTAs: "View my work" | "Follow the journey"
```

**Why:** Removes years as a metric, shifts focus from enterprise metrics to builder identity and sharing.

## IDENTITY REINFORCED

Your website now consistently communicates:

✓ **I'm a builder** - focused on solving real problems
✓ **I'm learning** - honest about the journey
✓ **I'm sharing** - inviting others to learn with me
✓ **I'm authentic** - no fluff, no exaggeration
✓ **I'm technical** - specific stack (.NET, MAUI, React)
✓ **I'm human** - personality and genuine connection

This positioning works for:
- **Developers seeking mentorship** - from someone real, not corporate
- **Tech students learning** - from authentic experience
- **Curious engineers** - interested in the process, not the pitch
- **Recruiters** (secondary) - seeing genuine technical depth
